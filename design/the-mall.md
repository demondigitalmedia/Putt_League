# Putt League: The Mall After Hours (design)

Live design doc (edit there first): https://claude.ai/artifact/U8r1rBKPZf3pWJKAhqaMQ1

A shuttered mall taken over after hours by a putt league with total control of the space. Everything is handmade: fabric bolts, cardboard, inflatables, borrowed furniture, string lights, paint-marker signs. Inspired by the makeshift league in the old Jo-Ann Fabrics on Saginaw Highway, rebuilt with unlimited room, budget and time.

Two 18-hole courses:
- **The Stores**: nine stores, two holes each, starting in the fabric store. Par 50.
- **The Commons**: concourse, fountain court, atrium, food court, cinema, service corridor, parking deck, rooftop. Par 54.

Defaults for this draft: after hours lighting; fictional names (mall "Midtown Square", league "Putt League After Hours"); stacked baskets on some holes (lower = short putt, upper = long); same modes (Putt, Pitch, Unlimited).

## Art direction
- Materials: felt, burlap, quilted fabric, cardboard, plywood with visible screws, duct tape, zip ties, ratchet straps, pool noodles, foam board.
- Walls and lanes: fabric bolts on end, shower curtains on tension rods, folding tables on their sides, rolling clothes racks.
- Signs: cardboard and foam board, hand-lettered in paint marker, hung on twine; hole numbers on pizza boxes; bank boards on painted plywood with a gold rim.
- Lighting: dark mall, pools of warm light, string lights, clamp lights, a few neon store signs, one color gel per store, a blacklight zone with glow paint.
- Floors: mall tile (slick), store carpet (grabby), rubber gym mats, painted plywood, AstroTurf tee runners.
- Background life: players in folding chairs, a DJ table, a security guard on a Segway, a janitor's cart, a mall cat.

## New systems, in build order
1. Venue files: split each venue into its own file; move the Taproom first.
2. Level changes: real floor heights for balconies, escalators, steps.
3. Stacked baskets: two catchers on one pole.
4. Throw-through gates for openings (arch, TV hole, revolving door, tray window).
5. Moving and reactive objects: swings, carousel, glass elevator, toppling towers, rolling spools, wobbling inflatables.
6. Craft materials: felt, cardboard, plywood, fabric, foam, each with bounce, skip and sound.
7. Lighting per store: color gels, pools of light, blacklight glow, live TV wall.
8. Course picker: The Stores or The Commons.
